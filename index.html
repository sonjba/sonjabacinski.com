About

My career began in finance, where I completed a foundation degree in Financial Management and gained hands-on experience in accounts and reconciliation before moving into software engineering. That combination has become one of my greatest strengths. I understand both the code that moves financial data and the business context behind it.

Over the past year and a half, I worked at Exaloan, a fintech connecting institutional investors with loan originators. There, I built production-grade Python systems, including data pipelines, API integrations, and automated reconciliation solutions. In 2026, I completed my BSc in Computing at Birkbeck, University of London. My thesis, focused on graph databases for financial lending data, was developed in collaboration with Exaloan.

I’m now looking for opportunities in integration, data engineering, and automation within financial services. I’m particularly interested in solving complex problems involving manual processes or fragmented financial data, turning them into systems that are clean, validated and reliable.

  • Graph ETL Pipeline | BSc Thesis

    Challenge: In a lending ecosystem, loans, borrowers and investors are heavily interconnected. Stored as an operational NoSQL document store, though, those relationships aren't directly queryable, answering a "who's connected to what" question means reconstructing it through repeated lookups instead of a single traversal.

    Solution: Worked with developers and finance stakeholders at Exaloan to understand the underlying data and designed a graph schema that faithfully represented it, then built a 4,000+ line Python ETL pipeline transforming the loan-portfolio data into a property graph in ArangoDB (~58,000 nodes, ~110,000 edges, zero orphan edges), with deterministic hash-keyed idempotent ingestion, schema-drift detection, validation, and PII redaction. The thesis evaluates the graph as a derived analytical layer sitting alongside the existing document store, rather than replacing it.

    Code private (built with employer)

    • Python
    • ArangoDB
    • ETL

    Solution: Built an Excel-based invoicing system that generates invoices, tracks invoice numbers, exports to PDF, and maintains per-customer totals and payment tracking, consolidating everything into one clean master register.
